# Objective

Prevent unfair ticket hoarding and exploitative resale.

---

# Core Mechanisms

- hard cap pricing
- purchase limits
- ownership verification
- reputation score
- suspicious activity detection

---

# Hard Cap Rules

Maximum resale:
115% from original price.

---

# Purchase Limits

Users may only purchase:
2-4 tickets per event.

---

# Detection Signals

- abnormal pricing
- mass purchases
- repeated rapid resale
- multi-account behavior
- suspicious device patterns

---

# Enforcement Actions

- warning
- listing restriction
- temporary suspension
- permanent ban

---

# Technical Priorities

1. fairness
2. traceability
3. abuse prevention
4. marketplace trust
