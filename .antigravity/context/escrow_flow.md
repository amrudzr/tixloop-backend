# Escrow Flow

buyer purchases ticket
↓
payment received
↓
funds locked in escrow
↓
ticket ownership transferred
↓
buyer validates ticket
↓
venue check-in success
↓
funds released to seller

---

# Dispute Flow

ticket disputed
↓
escrow frozen
↓
evidence reviewed
↓
resolution executed

---

# Escrow Objectives

- reduce fraud
- prevent fake resale
- protect buyers
- maintain trust

---

# Release Triggers

- successful venue validation
- buyer confirmation
- auto release timeout
- admin resolution
