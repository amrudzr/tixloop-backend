# Resale Flow

seller uploads ticket
↓
ticket ownership verified
↓
listing created
↓
marketplace discovery
↓
buyer purchases
↓
escrow initiated
↓
ownership transferred
↓
ticket validated
↓
transaction completed

---

# Listing Rules

- one active listing per ticket
- verified ownership required
- hard cap enforced
- floor price enforced

---

# Marketplace Priorities

1. trust
2. transparency
3. fairness
4. fast circulation
