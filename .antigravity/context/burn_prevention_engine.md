# Burn Prevention Engine

## Objective

Reduce ticket waste by automatically improving ticket circulation near event dates.

---

# Core Concept

As the event date approaches:
ticket resale prices decrease automatically.

---

# Goals

- reduce unused tickets
- improve affordability
- improve resale success rate
- reduce economic waste

---

# Core Mechanism

Initial Price
↓
Timed Price Reduction
↓
Floor Price Protection
↓
Last Minute Circulation

---

# Price Drop Logic

Example:
- drop every 8 hours
- reduce by 8%
- stop at floor price

---

# Floor Price

Minimum allowed resale value.

Example:
45% from original ticket price.

---

# Hard Cap

Maximum resale price:
115% from original ticket price.

Purpose:
anti-scalper enforcement.

---

# Marketplace Benefits

- fair pricing
- faster circulation
- reduced waste
- improved accessibility

---
