# Ticket Lifecycle

## Objective

Define complete ticket ownership and resale flow.

---

# Lifecycle States

created
↓
owned
↓
listed
↓
in_escrow
↓
sold
↓
validated
↓
used

---

# State Definitions

## created
Ticket generated by organizer.

## owned
Ticket belongs to current owner.

## listed
Ticket actively listed on marketplace.

## in_escrow
Transaction initiated and funds held.

## sold
Ownership transferred successfully.

## validated
Ticket validated at venue.

## used
Ticket consumed during event entry.

---

# Invalid States

## burned
Ticket expired unused.

## disputed
Ownership or validity problem detected.

## suspended
Ticket temporarily blocked.

---

# Lifecycle Priorities

- ownership integrity
- fraud prevention
- auditability
- safe transfer
- circular reuse

---

# Core Rules

- one active owner only
- one active listing only
- escrow before ownership transfer
- ownership history immutable

---

# Lifecycle Objectives

- prevent double selling
- prevent fake resale
- reduce ticket waste
- improve circulation
