# Planning Structure

Every planning output must include:

## Objective
What needs to be achieved.

---

## Scope
What is included and excluded.

---

## Dependencies
Required systems, modules, or prerequisites.

---

## Risks
Potential blockers or instability.

---

## Technical Considerations
Architecture or implementation impact.

---

## Priority Level

Allowed:
- critical
- important
- optional
- future_scope
