## 1. Planning Policy
*   **MVP Alignment**: Prioritize features essential for OLIVIA demo stability. Non-essential features (e.g. advanced AI, blockchain, custom notifications) must be cataloged under future/optional work.
*   **Execution Checks**: Before initiating coding, planning logs must evaluate technical dependencies and risk areas.
